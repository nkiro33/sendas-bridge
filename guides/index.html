Setup guides

Pick the email provider that will send your mail. If you don't have one, Resend is the quickest to set up and Brevo has the biggest free plan.

ProviderFree planGuide
Resend
Developer-friendly, quick domain setup. Good default.
3,000 emails a month free (100 a day)Set up Resend →
Brevo
Generous free plan. Pasted images arrive as attachments, not inline.
300 emails a day freeSet up Brevo →
SMTP2GO
Popular with former Gmail "Send mail as" users. Simple verified-sender setup.
1,000 emails a month free (200 a day)Set up SMTP2GO →
Mailgun
Sends the exact message SendAs Bridge builds (raw MIME).
100 emails a day on the free planSet up Mailgun →

Cheapest at volume. New accounts start in a sandbox until AWS approves production access.
About $0.10 per 1,000 emails (3,000 a month free for the first 12 months)Set up Amazon SES →
Postmark
Excellent deliverability for personal mail. Paid plans from $15/month.
100 emails a month free; new accounts need approval before sending to anyoneSet up Postmark →
