Send from your own domain in Gmail with Anypost

Setup guide · about 15 minutes, plus approval · 3,000 emails a month free while in evaluation (to your own domains only), then from $0.10 per 1,000

Gmail is ending “Send mail as” for addresses it doesn't host (Google Help). With Anypost and SendAs Bridge you keep writing in Gmail, and your email is sent from you@yourdomain.com through your own Anypost account.

Vetted sending network. New accounts need a short approval before mailing other people.

1. Set up Anypost

  1. Create an account at anypost.com.
  2. In the dashboard, add your domain (for example yourdomain.com) and publish the CNAME records it shows: one for verification and two for DKIM. Anypost handles SPF and DKIM behind them. Verify the domain.
  3. Create an API key. "Send only" is enough. Copy the secret when it is shown (it starts with ap_); Anypost shows it only once.
  4. To send to people outside your own domain, fill in the short application from the banner in the dashboard. Anypost usually replies within one business day.
Until Anypost approves your account it refuses any email with a recipient outside your own verified domains. That includes the copy SendAs Bridge sends to your Gmail, so finish the application before you rely on it.
Each email can be about 3.5 MB of attachments at most.
If your DMARC record uses strict alignment (adkim=s or aspf=s), relax it, or mail sent through Anypost may fail DMARC.

2. Connect it to SendAs Bridge

  1. Install SendAs Bridge for Edge or for Firefox. The setup page opens automatically.
  2. Choose Anypost and fill in:
    • API key: Anypost dashboard → API keys → Create. "Send only" is enough; a full key also lets SendAs Bridge check your domain.
  3. Click Connect. Your browser asks to let SendAs Bridge reach Anypost's API. Click Allow. The key is checked right away.
  4. Add your address (for example you@yourdomain.com) and your name.
  5. Run the domain check and fix anything marked ❌, then send yourself a test. Open it in Gmail: SendAs Bridge shows Gmail's SPF, DKIM and DMARC verdict.

3. Send from Gmail

Open Gmail, start a new email or a reply, and pick your address in the From row at the top of the compose window. Replies to mail sent to your domain select it automatically. Click Send; a copy appears in your Gmail conversation.

What's supported with Anypost

  • Inline pictures: yes, sent as real inline images
  • Reply threading: headers are sent; not yet confirmed with this provider
  • Maximum email size: about 5 MB, up to 50 recipients
